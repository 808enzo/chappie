# Control metric: how to read the held share

`SKILL.md` defines the held share: a conclusion, held and not held, the re-read deadline, the denominator,
a definition fixed because it was wrong, and the share as a line of the report itself, with a worked
example; why conclusions and not lines, why not the share of metrics with a written definition, how the
share rises when the work gets worse, and the three numbers that catch it. This file holds what moves it,
three more numbers to read beside it, and what it cannot see.

## What moves it

**Where it moves.** Down when conclusions are published on immature cohort cells and unsettled returns,
when a join or an export breaks, when a holdout contradicts attribution, when nobody re-reads. Up with
the steps in the three mechanic files.

## Read beside it

**Three more numbers to read beside the held share, and promote none.**

- **Coverage:** the program areas whose control metric carries a conclusion in the cycle's report, over all
  program areas.
- **The cause of every conclusion that did not hold:** settling, error, attribution, not re-read. Each has a
  different repair.
- **The residuals of the checks:** double credit, and the gap to revenue in accounting.

## What it cannot see

**What it cannot see.** A correct line nobody needs; the decision column in the report catches that. A
conclusion that held because the same error sat in the line at publication and at re-reading; the check
against accounting and the sample of rows behind a money line catch that.
