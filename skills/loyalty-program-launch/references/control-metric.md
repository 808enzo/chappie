# Control metric: what the average hides, and the diagnostic signals

`SKILL.md` defines the control metric, the share of transactions identified to a known customer: why this
number, the two classes that sit in its denominator by construction, why not the share of the base
enrolled, and the self-baseline. This file holds what an average across locations hides, and where the two
diagnostic signals are read.

## What the average hides

Average penetration hides how far apart the locations are, and the work is with the lower group.

## Diagnostic signals

Active member share and redemption share are diagnostic signals, both read in
`first-months-and-course-correction.md`: the first together with new customer inflow, the second
together with the liability.
