# Getting help

One maintainer runs Chappie, and all help happens in public on GitHub, so the next person with the
same question finds the answer.

## Where to ask

| You want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Find the skill that fits your case, or understand a step | [Discussions, Q&A category](https://github.com/808enzo/chappie/discussions/new?category=q-a) |
| Report a changed rule, a skill that gets something wrong, or a failed installation | [An issue](https://github.com/808enzo/chappie/issues/new/choose) |
| Request a missing mechanic or a new skill | [An issue](https://github.com/808enzo/chappie/issues/new/choose) |
| Report behavior that breaks the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | The contact in its Enforcement section, not a public thread |

Search Discussions and issues before you post: your question may already have an answer.
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains what each issue form asks for.

Discussions and issues are public. Remove customer and employee data before you post, as
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#before-you-post) describes.

## What you won't get here

- **Private support.** Questions sent by email or direct message get no answer. The conduct
  contact takes conduct reports only.
- **Setup help for one platform.** How to configure a specific email platform, CDP or CRM belongs
  in that vendor's documentation.
- **Market benchmarks.** A question about the normal open rate or churn rate gets no figure.
  [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md#on-numbers) explains why.
- **A response time.** The maintainer answers Discussions and reviews issues without a deadline.
